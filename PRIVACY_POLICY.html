Privacy Policy for Roklaro

Last updated: June 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Roklaro ("the app," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application.

Who We Are

Roklaro is developed by an individual developer (Mathias Skartved) based in Denmark. Roklaro is a fitness and health literacy app designed for users aged 15 and up, teaching short lessons on sleep, food, movement, mindset, and recovery.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide the app's core functionality:

  • Email address: used to create and authenticate your account.
  • Username (optional): if you choose to set a username, it is displayed in the app instead of your email address. This is optional and can be left blank.
  • Lesson and quiz progress: we store which lessons you've completed, your answers to quiz questions, whether those answers were correct, and your accumulated points. This is used to track your progress, sync it across your devices, and show you your personal accuracy statistics within the app.

We do not collect your name, date of birth, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information beyond what is listed above. Roklaro does not use any analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Save and sync your lesson progress and quiz results across devices
  • Display your progress and accuracy statistics back to you within the app
  • Send you occasional in-app messages (such as a welcome message) related to using the app

We do not sell your information to third parties. We do not use your information for advertising.

Third-Party Services

Roklaro uses Supabase as its backend service provider for authentication and data storage. Supabase processes the information described above on our behalf, under their own privacy and security practices. You can review Supabase's privacy policy at https://supabase.com/privacy.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase's infrastructure, with row-level security policies ensuring that only you can access your own account data. We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account information and progress data for as long as your account remains active. If you would like your account and associated data deleted, please contact us at roklaroapp@outlook.com and we will process your request within a reasonable timeframe.

Children's Privacy

Roklaro is intended for users aged 15 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
