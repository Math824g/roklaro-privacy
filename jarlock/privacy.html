Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Jarlock ("the app") helps you lock chosen apps during times you choose. It is built to know as little about you as possible.

What we collect

Nothing. Jarlock has no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Your jars (which apps, which times) are stored only on your phone.

iPhone: Screen Time

On iPhone, Jarlock uses Apple's Screen Time API (FamilyControls, ManagedSettings, DeviceActivity). Apple only gives Jarlock opaque tokens for the apps you pick — Jarlock cannot see which apps they are, and no usage data leaves your device.

Android: Accessibility Service

On Android, Jarlock uses the Accessibility Service API for one purpose: to detect when you open an app that you placed in a locked jar, so it can close it and show the lock screen. The service only receives "window changed" events from the apps in your jars. It does not read the content of your screen, does not perform actions in other apps, and does not collect, store or send any personal data. You can turn it off at any time in Settings → Accessibility.

Jarlock reads the list of installed apps only to let you pick apps for a jar. This list never leaves your phone.

Purchases

If you buy Jarlock Pro, payment is handled by the App Store or Google Play. We receive no payment card details. We may use RevenueCat to confirm your subscription status, which processes an anonymous purchase identifier.

Children

Jarlock is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
