Account and Data Deletion for Roklaro

Last updated: June 30, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Roklaro account, or deletion of specific data, from the Roklaro app ("the app," "we," "us").

How to Request Account Deletion

To delete your Roklaro account, email us at roklaroapp@outlook.com with the following:

  • Your account username (or, if you have not set a username, the email address you use to sign in to Roklaro)
  • The words "account deletion" so we know exactly what you are requesting

We will verify that the request comes from the account owner before deleting anything.

How to Request Deletion of Specific Data

If you want to delete certain data without deleting your whole account, email us at the same address, roklaroapp@outlook.com, with your account username (or sign-in email) and a clear description of which data you would like removed (for example, your lesson and quiz progress). We will delete the data you specify and keep the rest of your account active.

What Gets Deleted

When you request full account deletion, we permanently delete:

  • Your account and the email address associated with it
  • Your username
  • Your lesson and quiz progress, including your answers, accuracy statistics, and accumulated points
  • All other data associated with your account

Your data is stored on our behalf by our backend provider, Supabase, and deletion removes it there as well. We do not sell your information to third parties, and we do not use your information for advertising, so there are no third-party copies of your data sold or shared for those purposes to recover or delete elsewhere.

How Long It Takes

We process deletion requests within 30 days of receiving them. In most cases it is done sooner. If we need to confirm anything with you first, we will reply to the email address you contacted us from.

Contact Us

For any account or data deletion request, or questions about this process, contact us at:
